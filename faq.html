Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our building survey services.

What is a Building Survey (Level 3)?

A Building Survey is the most thorough and detailed report available on the condition of a property. It's carried out by a Chartered Surveyor and covers the structure, construction and condition of the building in depth, including advice on defects and their likely causes.

Do I need a Level 2 or Level 3 survey?

A Level 2 HomeBuyer Report suits conventional properties in reasonable condition. A Level 3 Building Survey is recommended for older homes, properties that have been altered or extended, or if you're planning renovation work. Our team can advise based on your property's age and condition, free of charge.

How much does a survey cost?

Prices depend on the property's size, age, value and location. As a guide, Level 1 surveys start from £350, Level 2 from £450 and Level 3 from £650. Complete our contact form for a free, no-obligation estimate for your specific property.

How long does a survey take?

The on-site inspection typically takes 2–4 hours depending on the size and type of survey. Your written report is usually delivered within 5 working days of the inspection.

Can I attend the survey?

Yes — many of our clients like to meet the surveyor at the property. We recommend joining towards the end of the inspection for a walk-through summary of key findings.

Will the report include photographs?

Yes, our reports include relevant photographs to help illustrate key findings alongside the written commentary.

Are your surveyors RICS qualified?

Yes. All Byrne & Co surveys are carried out by surveyors holding MRICS or FRICS accreditation from the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors, and we carry full professional indemnity insurance.

Does a Building Survey include a valuation?

No — a Building Survey focuses on condition and construction, not market value. If you need a valuation alongside your survey, our Level 2 HomeBuyer Report can include one, or we can arrange a separate valuation.

What areas do you cover?

We cover all London boroughs and the surrounding M25 corridor. See our Areas We Cover page for the full list.

Further FAQs for London Homebuyers

What happens if my survey finds a serious defect?

We'll flag anything urgent as soon as possible rather than waiting for the full report, and talk you through your options, which usually include renegotiating the price, requesting repairs, or getting a specialist to investigate further before you exchange.

Can I be present during the property inspection?

Yes, many London clients join us for the last 15-20 minutes of the inspection for an informal walk-through, though it isn't required as everything is covered in your written report and follow-up call.

Does my mortgage lender need a copy of the survey?

Our report is for you as the buyer; if your lender requires their own valuation that's usually arranged separately, but we're happy to talk through our findings if it helps your conversation with them.

What if I'm unhappy with the report or service?

As RICS-regulated surveyors we follow the RICS complaints handling procedure, so if you have concerns we'll aim to resolve them directly, with RICS's independent Dispute Resolution Service available as a further step.

Do you provide a fixed appointment time for the inspection?

Yes, we'll agree a specific appointment window with you in advance, and our surveyor will confirm timing ahead of the visit so you can plan around it if you'd like to be there.

Can I request a survey on a tight deadline before exchange?

We'll always try to accommodate urgent London transactions, get in touch with your timeline and we'll let you know the fastest realistic turnaround for your property.
