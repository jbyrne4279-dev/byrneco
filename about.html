About Byrne & Co Surveying

Independent, RICS chartered building surveyors serving homeowners and buyers across London.

Who We Are

Byrne & Co Surveying is an independent firm of Chartered Surveyors, regulated by the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS). We specialise exclusively in residential building surveys — Level 1 Condition Reports, Level 2 HomeBuyer Reports and Level 3 Building Surveys — for homeowners and buyers across Greater London.

Every survey is carried out in person by a qualified surveyor holding MRICS or FRICS accreditation, giving you confidence that the report you receive meets professional RICS standards. We turn down work outside our expertise rather than stretch a generalist practice across every property type — building surveys in London are all we do.

Why Choose Us

  • RICS Chartered Surveyors (MRICS/FRICS)
  • Professional indemnity insured
  • Plain-English reports with photographs
  • Local knowledge of London property types
  • Reports delivered within 5 working days
  • Free follow-up call to talk through findings

How We Work

We keep the same surveyor with you from the initial call through to the post-report discussion, rather than routing you through a call centre. That continuity matters when you're deciding whether a crack is cosmetic or structural, or whether a damp reading needs a specialist follow-up.

Our Approach

We believe a survey should give you clarity, not just a stack of paperwork.

Thorough Inspection

We inspect every accessible part of the property, from roof void to sub-floor, using the appropriate level of survey for your needs.

Clear Reporting

Our reports use plain English and colour-coded ratings so you understand exactly what's been found and what to do about it.

Ongoing Support

After delivery, we're available by phone or email to talk through the findings and help you plan next steps.
