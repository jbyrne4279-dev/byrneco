Coverage FAQs

FAQs About Our London Coverage

Do you cover properties inside the M25 as well as central London?

Yes, we survey properties across every London borough plus the wider M25 corridor, so whether you're buying in Zone 1 or on the outer edge of Greater London, we can help.

Is there an extra charge for surveys in central London postcodes?

No, our pricing is based on the property itself, its size, age and condition, not its postcode, so a flat in Zone 1 is quoted the same way as a house in outer London.

How quickly can you book a survey in my area?

Most London postcodes can be booked within a few working days, and your written report follows within 5 working days of the inspection, wherever in the capital the property is.

Do you know the local property types in my borough?

Our surveyors regularly work across all 32 London boroughs, so we're familiar with the common construction types and typical defects in your specific area, from Victorian terraces to 1930s semis and modern conversions.
