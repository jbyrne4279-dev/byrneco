Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Byrne & Co Surveying ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and your rights.

What We Collect

When you submit our contact form we collect your name, email address, telephone number, property postcode and details about the survey you're enquiring about.

How We Use It

We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry, provide a survey estimate, and, where you proceed, to arrange and deliver your survey. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry data only as long as necessary to respond to you and fulfil any survey booking, and in line with our legal and insurance obligations.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or request deletion of your personal data. Contact us at enquiries@byrneco-surveying.co.uk to exercise these rights.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to enquiries@byrneco-surveying.co.uk or by phone on 0800 0147 453.
