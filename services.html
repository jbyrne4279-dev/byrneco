Our Survey Services

Every survey is carried out by a RICS chartered surveyor, following the RICS Home Survey Standard.

Level 3 Building Survey

A Building Survey is the most thorough and detailed report you can commission on the condition of a property. It is carried out by a Chartered Surveyor (MRICS/FRICS) and is often referred to as a Full Structural Survey.

It is recommended for older properties, homes that have been extensively altered, dilapidated buildings, or if you're planning to renovate or convert. Unlike a valuation, a Building Survey focuses purely on the condition and construction of the property, identifying defects, their likely causes, and recommended repairs.

  • Full structural inspection, roof to foundations
  • Detailed defect analysis with causes & remedies
  • Advice on repair costs and maintenance priorities
  • Optional photographic annex

Who is it for?

Older houses (pre-1930s), listed buildings, timber-framed or non-standard construction, properties with visible defects, or any home you plan to extend or renovate.

Who is it for?

Conventional homes built from common materials in reasonable condition, generally under 50 years old, where major problems are considered unlikely.

Level 2 HomeBuyer Report

A HomeBuyer Report follows a standard format set by the RICS and sits between a basic valuation and a full Building Survey. It gives a clear picture of the property's condition, highlights urgent defects, and can include a market valuation.

  • Condition ratings for all major elements
  • Identification of urgent and significant defects
  • Optional market valuation & rebuild cost
  • Easy-to-read traffic-light format

RICS Level 1 Home Survey (Condition Report)

Our most cost-effective option, and typically the lowest-priced of the three. A Level 1 Home Survey describes the property's condition, identifies any risks and potential legal issues, and highlights urgent defects using a simple traffic-light rating — but it doesn't advise on how to manage those defects or what repairs might cost. It's a standard visual inspection, so secured panels, electrical fittings and inspection chamber covers aren't removed.

  • Visual-only inspection, no repair or maintenance advice
  • Traffic-light condition ratings
  • Fastest turnaround, lowest cost
Get a Level 1 Estimate

Who is it for?

Conventional houses, flats and bungalows built from common building materials and already in good condition — typically new-build and modern homes — where you mainly want independent confirmation before you buy, rather than detailed repair guidance.
