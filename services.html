Our Survey Services

Every survey is carried out by a RICS chartered surveyor, following the RICS Home Survey Standard.

RICS Level 1 Home Survey (Condition Report)

Our most cost-effective option. A visual-only check of the property's condition, flagging urgent defects and legal risks with a simple traffic-light rating.

  • Visual-only inspection, no repair or maintenance advice
  • Traffic-light condition ratings
  • Fastest turnaround, lowest cost
Get Quote

Who is it for?

Conventional houses, flats and bungalows built from common building materials and already in good condition, typically new-build and modern homes, where you mainly want independent confirmation before you buy rather than detailed repair guidance.

RICS Level 2 Home Survey (HomeBuyer Report)

Previously called a "HomeBuyer Report", this sits between Level 1 and Level 3: everything in Level 1, plus maintenance advice and an extended roof and drainage inspection.

  • Survey only: condition ratings, defect and maintenance advice, extended roof space and drainage inspection
  • Survey and valuation: the above, plus a market valuation and insurance reinstatement figure
Get Quote

Who is it for?

Conventional homes built from common materials, in reasonable condition, and simple in form and layout, generally under 50 years old, without multiple alterations or extensions.

RICS Level 3 Home Survey (Building Survey)

Formerly known as a "Building Survey", this is our most comprehensive report. It covers how the property is built, what condition it's in, and what any issues will cost to fix.

  • Thorough inspection of the roof space, grounds, floors and services
  • Construction type, materials and how they'll perform
  • Visible and potential hidden defects
  • Repair options, timelines and cost of inaction
  • Optional photographic annex
Get Quote

Who is it for?

Large, older or run-down properties, buildings that are unusual or have been altered, listed buildings, timber-framed or non-standard construction, or anywhere you're planning major works.

Choosing a Survey

FAQs on Picking the Right London Survey

How do I know if I need a Level 2 or Level 3 survey in London?

As a rule of thumb, a Level 2 HomeBuyer Report suits conventional homes under about 50 years old in reasonable condition, while a Level 3 Building Survey is recommended for older, altered or unusual London properties, which covers most of the capital's period housing stock.

How much does a building survey cost in London?

Prices typically start from £350 for a Level 1 Condition Report, £450 for a Level 2 HomeBuyer Report and £650 for a Level 3 Building Survey, though the exact cost depends on the property's size, value and condition.

Can I upgrade from a Level 2 to a Level 3 survey later?

Yes, just let us know before the inspection and we'll rebook the appointment for the more detailed Level 3 Building Survey rather than duplicating a visit.

Do you survey both houses and flats across London?

Yes, we survey houses, flats, maisonettes and new-build apartments throughout Greater London, adjusting the inspection to cover shared structures on leasehold properties where relevant.
