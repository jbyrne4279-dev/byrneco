Which Survey Do You Need?

Not sure whether you need a Level 2 or Level 3 survey? Our team will help you choose the right report for your property, free of charge.

Level 1 Survey

A basic visual condition report, ideal for newer, conventional properties in good condition where you need a quick, cost-effective overview.

  • Visual inspection only
  • Traffic-light condition ratings
  • Best for new-build / modern homes

From £350

Most Popular

Level 2 — HomeBuyer Report

A mid-tier survey suitable for conventional properties in reasonable condition, giving more detail than a Level 1 with a valuation option.

  • Detailed condition ratings
  • Optional market valuation
  • Best for standard homes under 50 years old

From £450

Level 3 — Building Survey

The most comprehensive report available, recommended for older, larger, altered or run-down properties, or if you plan to renovate.

  • In-depth structural analysis
  • Defects, causes & repair advice
  • Best for older or non-standard homes

From £650
