FAQs About Our Homeowner Guides

Are these guides specific to London properties?

Yes, every guide is written around the housing stock and risks typical of Greater London, from Victorian terraces and clay subsidence to leasehold flats and new-build snagging.

Do the guides replace getting a professional survey?

No, they're designed to help you understand what a survey covers and what to expect, but they aren't a substitute for an in-person inspection by a RICS chartered surveyor.

How often are the guides updated?

We review and update our guides regularly to reflect current RICS Home Survey Standard practice and typical London market conditions.

Can you write a guide on a specific topic I'm researching?

Get in touch and let us know what you're trying to find out, we're happy to point you to the right guide or answer your question directly.

Do you have a guide on survey costs across London?

Yes, our complete guide to building surveys in London covers typical pricing for each survey level alongside what's included.

Are the guides written by RICS chartered surveyors?

Yes, our guides are written and reviewed by our own RICS chartered surveying team, drawing on what we see inspecting London properties day to day.
